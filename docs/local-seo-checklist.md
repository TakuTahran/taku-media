# Local SEO checklist (off-site)

On-page work on this site is only part of local ranking. Complete these yourself. Do not invent NAP details in the codebase until they are confirmed.

## Google Business Profile

- [ ] Create or claim a Google Business Profile as a **service-area business** if there is no public storefront.
- [ ] Set primary category carefully (e.g. business consultant / IT services consultant). Add secondary categories only if accurate.
- [ ] Service areas: Montreal and the municipalities you actually serve. Do not claim the entire province unless true.
- [ ] Description in clear language. Avoid keyword stuffing. Match the positioning on the site (implementation and integration, not a platform vendor).
- [ ] Photos: real workspace, headshot, or project diagrams you own. No stock "AI brain" imagery.
- [ ] Products/services: align names with published service pages only.
- [ ] Enable messaging or booking only if you will respond promptly. Link booking to `https://cal.com/tahranc` if that remains the source of truth.

## NAP consistency

- [ ] Confirm legal business name, phone, and mailing or registered address.
- [ ] Use the **exact same** name, address, phone (NAP) on every citation.
- [ ] Update the site TODOs (`confirm NAP`) only after that confirmation.
- [ ] Match GBP, website footer, email signatures, invoices, and LinkedIn company page.

## Quebec and Montreal citations

- [ ] Chambre de commerce de Montréal / relevant chamber membership if you join.
- [ ] Quebec business directories you trust (verify each before creating a listing).
- [ ] Industry directories for consultants / digital agencies only if the listing is accurate.
- [ ] Avoid low-quality citation farms. Fewer clean listings beat dozens of inconsistent ones.

## Reviews

- [ ] Ask for reviews only from real clients after completed work.
- [ ] Prefer Google reviews for local SEO; LinkedIn recommendations help trust but are secondary for Maps.
- [ ] Never buy reviews or offer incentives that violate Google policies.
- [ ] Respond to every review, short and factual.

## Measurement

- [ ] Link GBP to Analytics / Search Console where available.
- [ ] Track calls, form submits, and Cal.com bookings as conversions.
- [ ] Recheck NAP and GBP categories quarterly.

## Bill 96 / Charter of the French Language

Confirm your obligations with counsel for your specific situation. Site structure supports `/en` and `/fr`. French commercial communications requirements are legal, not a design preference.
