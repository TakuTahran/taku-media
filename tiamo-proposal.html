Content Strategy Proposal

TI AMO ENTERTAINMENT Group

Content strategy proposal to increase social media activity and brand awareness.

Contact: (514) 239-7117

1. Discovery & Goals

Before creating content, we want to understand why TI AMO wants to increase social media activity.

Key questions:

  • Is the goal brand awareness — to make more people recognize TI AMO?
  • Is it to book more events (weddings, clubs, corporate, etc.)?
  • Do you want to showcase your DJs and event setups to build credibility?
  • Is the goal to grow your online following or increase inquiries through Instagram/TikTok?
  • Are there specific locations or audiences you want to reach (Montreal nightlife, wedding planners, etc.)?

2. Proposed Content Strategy

We propose focusing on short-form video content (Reels & TikToks), highlighting energy, visuals, and experience.

Types of content:

  • Event Highlights: Showcase the crowd, lighting, music, and DJ performance.
  • Behind-the-Scenes: Setup, sound check, and prep moments.
  • DJ Personality Clips: Fun, relatable moments with the team.
  • Client Testimonials: Quick clips from happy couples or event organizers.
  • Trend Adaptations: Reels/TikToks using trending sounds that match your vibe.

Tone & Style

High-energy, cinematic, modern — appealing to party-goers, event planners, and nightlife lovers.

3. Production Plan

  • Format: Vertical (9:16) optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  • Filming: At live events or dedicated shoots.
  • Editing: Fast cuts, dynamic captions, and branded motion graphics.
