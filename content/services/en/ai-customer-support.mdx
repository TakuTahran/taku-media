AI customer support Montreal companies need is deflection of repeatable tier-1 work without trapping angry customers in a loop. We set up agents on the help desk you already use, with escalation that a human can trust.

## A concrete support agent workflow

1. Cluster last quarter's tickets into intents you can name.
2. Write answer sources from approved help center articles only.
3. Launch in suggest mode: agent drafts, agents send.
4. Promote high-confidence intents to auto-reply with a visible "talk to a person" path.
5. Log every containment miss and retrain weekly from those misses.

Channels can include chat, email, and social inboxes. The rules stay the same: cite sources, escalate on refunds, legal, safety, or customer sentiment that turns sharp.

## Worked example: shipping status vs refunds

A retailer wanted full automation on day one. Ticket history showed shipping status was 35% of volume and almost always factual. Refunds were emotional and policy-heavy.

We automated shipping status with order lookups, and forced human handoff on refund language. Containment rose on the factual slice. Refund quality did not drop because the bot never negotiated.

## Constraint and tradeoff

Auto-reply without a help center is the bot inventing policy. If articles are stale, fix documentation before model work. That sequence feels slow and prevents public corrections later.

Multichannel also costs focus. One solid channel beats three half-wired ones. We usually start where volume and tooling already exist, then expand.
