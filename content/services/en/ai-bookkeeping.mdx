AI bookkeeping Montreal operators ask for is not a replacement accountant. It is faster categorization, cleaner feeds into the ledger, and earlier signals when cash looks thin. The books stay yours. The tools stay QuickBooks, Xero, or whatever you already run.

## A concrete bookkeeping assist workflow

1. Card and bank feeds land in the spend tool.
2. Rules plus a model suggest categories and vendors.
3. Bookkeeper or owner approves in batches, not line by line when confidence is high.
4. Approved lines sync to the ledger with a clear audit trail.
5. A weekly cash snapshot compares expected receivables and known bills.

We wire the glue. We do not become your controller of record.

## Worked example: messy vendor names

A studio had 40 variations of the same software vendor across three cards. Month-end cleanup ate an afternoon.

We added normalization rules and a review queue for novel vendors only. Repeat charges auto-suggested the same category. The afternoon became a 20-minute exception pass. Novel vendors still need a human. That is intentional.

## Constraint and tradeoff

Auto-posting without review is how bad categories become permanent and tax season gets expensive. We keep humans on approval for anything that hits the ledger.

Forecasting is another tradeoff. Short horizon cash views from open invoices and scheduled bills are useful. Long-range "AI predictions" without sales pipeline truth are decorative. We will say so rather than ship a chart that lies politely.
