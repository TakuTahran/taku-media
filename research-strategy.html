Research & Strategy

Opportunity mapping, journeys, IA, roadmaps.

Our research and strategy services help you understand your market, users, and opportunities. We combine quantitative data with qualitative insights to build a comprehensive understanding of your business landscape.

What we do

  • Discovery sprints: Intensive workshops to uncover opportunities and align stakeholders.
  • UX research: User interviews, surveys, and usability testing to understand behavior.
  • Analytics & KPIs: Data analysis to identify patterns and measure success.
  • Information architecture: Organize content and features for optimal user experience.
  • Roadmapping: Strategic planning to align product development with business goals.

Approach

We begin with a discovery phase to understand your business context, followed by research to uncover user needs and market opportunities. Our strategic framework aligns business objectives with user needs to create actionable roadmaps.

Deliverables

  • Research reports
  • User personas
  • Journey maps
  • Strategic roadmap
  • Success metrics
  • Opportunity matrix
