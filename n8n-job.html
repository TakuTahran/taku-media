n8n Automation Specialist

Taku Media

Company Description

Taku Media specializes in providing a Website as a Service solution designed to transform businesses. Our offering ensures that clients unlock a high-performing, visually appealing website without requiring technical expertise or maintenance concerns. This scalable solution grows alongside businesses, helping them maintain a strong and impactful online presence.

Role Description

The n8n Automation Specialist will be responsible for designing and implementing workflow automations using n8n, ensuring the efficiency and reliability of automated processes. Day-to-day tasks include troubleshooting automation issues, quality assurance checks, and creating test cases to verify functionality. This is a remote role that offers flexibility while working collaboratively with the team to optimize and maintain automation frameworks.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, implement, and maintain complex automation workflows in n8n
  • Integrate various applications, APIs, and databases to automate business processes
  • Troubleshoot, optimize, and document existing workflows
  • Collaborate with team members to identify automation opportunities and improve efficiency
  • Ensure reliability, scalability, and security of automated processes

Qualifications

  • Proven experience with n8n workflow automation (3+ years preferred)
  • Strong understanding of APIs, webhooks, and data integration
  • Experience with tools such as Google Workspace, Airtable, Slack, Notion, Shopify, or other SaaS platforms
  • Ability to troubleshoot workflow issues and optimize performance
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work independently or as part of a team

Nice-to-Have

  • Experience with other automation tools (Zapier, Make/Integromat, HubSpot workflows)
  • Familiarity with coding or scripting (JavaScript, Python, etc.) for custom workflow nodes
  • Knowledge of business process analysis and workflow optimization

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation
  • Fully remote work environment
  • Opportunity to work with cutting-edge automation tools
  • Collaborative and supportive team culture

How to Apply

Please submit your resume and a brief description of your experience with n8n automation workflows. Include examples of workflows you've built or challenges you've solved if possible.
